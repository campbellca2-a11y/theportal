Understanding Dawn and Dusk

Civil Dawn / Dusk

The sun is 6° below the horizon. Enough light to see objects without artificial light; horizon is clearly defined.

Nautical Dawn / Dusk

The sun is 12° below the horizon. Twilight is less pronounced; horizon is not clearly visible. Historically used for maritime navigation.

Astronomical Dawn / Dusk

The sun is 18° below the horizon. The last phase before full darkness. Used by astronomers to determine when it's dark enough for observations.

The Twilight Bands

The colorful gradient you see at the sunrise/sunset boundary shows all three twilight phases at once, sweeping across the globe as Earth rotates.
