03 / TRANSFER

Send it through.

Drop or choose a file on either device. Open or download it on the other. Keep the PC awake.

A little less cloud.

The website supplies the app. Your installed Portal moves files on your local network and keeps its inbox on your PC. It works without internet access after installation.

Up to 2 GB per file · 10 GB inbox. Removing a Portal copy leaves the original alone.

Know where it works.

This version connects devices on the same trusted home network. It does not send files between different homes. Local transfers use HTTP and are not encrypted.

If Windows asks for network access, allow Private networks on your trusted home network. Android device testing is still pending.
