**Drop it on your PC, grab it on your phone later.** No phone app, no cloud, no account.

### Install
1. Download `ThePortalSetup-…-windows-x64.exe` below and run it. No admin rights needed.
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Open **ThePortal** from the Start menu, click **Connect phone**, and scan the QR code with your phone (same Wi-Fi).

If Windows asks about network access, allow **Private networks**.

### In this version
- Up to 2 GB per file, 10 GB in the portal at once
- Files wait in the portal until you pick them up; the other device doesn't need to be open
- Works on your home Wi-Fi only (local HTTP, not encrypted)
- Windows 10/11 x64. iPhone tested; Android expected to work

The `.sha256` file lets you verify the download.
