THEPORTAL — Put it here. Get it there.

1. Open ThePortal from your desktop or Start menu.
2. Keep your PC and phone on the same trusted home network.
3. Click Connect phone on the PC, then scan its QR code with your phone.
4. Drop a file into either Portal. Open or download it on the other device.

The phone uses its browser; no phone app or account is required.
Keep the PC awake. Closing the browser leaves ThePortal running.
Use Start > ThePortal > Stop ThePortal when finished, after transfers complete.

If Windows asks about network access, allow only Private networks on a
trusted home network. Do not disable your firewall. Work-managed devices
may need an administrator to permit local network access.
If the phone cannot connect, check the network, PC sleep, VPN LAN settings,
and guest Wi-Fi isolation.

Files travel on your local network and are stored on this PC, not uploaded
to the public download site. This version uses unencrypted local HTTP;
pairing controls access but does not encrypt traffic.
100 MB per file; 1 GB inbox. Remove only Portal copies through the app.
Original files are untouched. Downloads already saved elsewhere remain.

Inbox location: %LOCALAPPDATA%\ThePortal\Data
Uninstalling preserves this inbox. A new install creates its own credentials;
it does not import files or pairing from older copies automatically.

Review build: unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher or reputation
warning. Do not disable security software. Broad public release awaits
Windows/phone acceptance testing and a signing decision.
